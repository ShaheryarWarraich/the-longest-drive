# The Longest Drive

The whole history of the universe (13.8 billion years) mapped onto one drive two kids know by heart:
their grandparents' house in Murree → the M2 → the Lahore Ring Road → home → up the stairs → the TV in the lounge.

**Play it:** open `index.html` in any browser. It is a single file — no internet needed, works from a USB stick, on a laptop or a phone in landscape.

- 1 km ≈ 36 million years · 1 ft ≈ 11,000 years · 1 inch ≈ 900 years · 1 mm ≈ 36 years
- Earth is born at the Sargodha exit, the first fish swim at the Airport exit, dinosaurs arrive just before our motorway exit, the first people appear in the upstairs passage 27 ft from the TV, and today is the screen itself.
- A second mode squeezes the same story into the 2026 calendar year.
- Every stop has a what-happened / how-it-happened card, and there is a printable set of cards to place around the house.

This public copy uses "Big Sister" and "Little Sister" and a plain schematic of a house; the family version has the real names, route and floor plan.
